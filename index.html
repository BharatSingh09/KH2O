About KH2O

Built for the sky,
engineered for the ground.

KH2O Drone Tech Solution builds autonomous aerial platforms that pair rugged, field-tested hardware with onboard artificial intelligence — so every drone we deploy doesn't just fly, it understands the terrain, the task, and the moment it's flying into.

From a single crop field to a national grid corridor, our systems are trained to detect, classify, and act, handing operators clear decisions instead of raw footage. We work directly with agriculture cooperatives, defence and security agencies, infrastructure contractors, power utilities, and logistics operators to put purpose-built drones into daily operational use.

01
Indigenous Engineering
Airframes, flight controllers, and payloads designed and assembled for Indian terrain and operating conditions.
02
AI at the Edge

Sector-First Design
Every payload and flight profile is built around the sector it serves, not retrofitted from a generic drone.

The AI Onboard
Every Flight.

Our flight software stack turns raw sensor feed into decisions the moment it's captured — not after the drone lands.

A1

Autonomous Navigation

Obstacle-aware path planning that re-routes mid-flight around terrain, structures, and no-fly zones without manual input.

A2

Real-Time Object Detection

Onboard computer vision models classify crops, structures, vehicles, and anomalies in-flight, at the edge, with no cloud round-trip needed.

A3

Predictive Analytics

Historical and live flight data feed models that forecast crop stress, equipment wear, and infrastructure risk before they escalate.

A4

Swarm Coordination

Multiple units share a common operating picture, splitting large survey or surveillance areas without overlapping flight paths.
